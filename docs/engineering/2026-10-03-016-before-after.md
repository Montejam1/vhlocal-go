## Document size

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| `SCAN_REPORT.md` bytes | 182,553 | **176,519** |
| `SCAN_REPORT.md` lines | 2,777 | **3,293** |
| `SCAN_REPORT.html` bytes | not recorded in the baseline | 226,567 |

The document is about 6 KB smaller but 516 lines longer. That is worth stating plainly
rather than presenting only the favourable half: the reading flow got much shorter (114
opening words instead of 557, 34 sections instead of 49), while each issue now carries five
labelled sections with real remediation steps and suggested checks where the old document
said "fix not provided", and every diagnostic that left the opening landed in Scan details
instead of being deleted. SC-010 measures consolidation, not length, and the consolidation is
what the numbers above show.

## What did not change

- Every one of the 49 supported detections is still present and is accounted for in exactly
  one primary issue — none lost, none double-counted.
- Every recorded evidence excerpt, citation range and classification is still in the
  document.
- The 8 dismissals and the 7 unsettled assessments keep their recorded reasons and decisions;
  the report never re-decides one.
- The three status axes stay independent. Execution reads "Incomplete", coverage reads 40 of
  100 completed, and the method reads "Source-code analysis"; none is derived from another.
- No model was called to produce any of this, and the target application was never executed.

## What this evidence does not establish

Passing offline tests and a measured document are not evidence that a reader understands the
report faster, that the page behaves well in a browser, or that it prints well. SC-001,
SC-008 and the browser and print review remain **not run** until an operator performs them
(see the quickstart's scenarios 20 through 22).
